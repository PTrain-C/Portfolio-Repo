## Where it is

I'm starting from IBM's Qiskit quantum magic 8-ball tutorial. It picks answers from measurements of qubits in superposition. Building from October 2026.

## Planned, not built

Real quantum entropy from IBM Quantum hardware through Qiskit Runtime, then an FPGA pipeline on a Digilent Basys 3 (Artix-7): health tests, a Toeplitz randomness extractor, FIFOs, and UART, with an on-chip ring-oscillator generator as a classical baseline. The goal is a real digital design project.

Simulator output is pseudorandom. Only bits from real quantum hardware count as quantum entropy.
