USC Racing builds a car for Formula SAE every year, and SCR26 is this year's. The rules require a Brake System Plausibility Device: a standalone circuit that cuts power if the throttle and hard braking show up together, or if a pedal sensor fails. That board was mine to design.

I kept it fully analog. The pedal signals are buffered so the BSPD and the ECU both see them without either loading the other, then compared against trimpot set points. An RC timer means a brief overlap won't trip it, but holding throttle through hard braking for about a second will, and once it trips it latches. A separate 100 ms path catches a sensor that's come unplugged. The full walkthrough, with the schematic broken into sections, is on the project page.

The board went through DRC and I produced the fabrication files. It wasn't installed or brought up on the car.
