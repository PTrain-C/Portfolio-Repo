I spent the summer as a systems engineering intern on the Aegis Combat System, the combat system that ties together radar, weapons, and command on Navy ships. Day to day that meant requirements work in IBM DOORS/Jazz and simulation testing in MATLAB.

## The intern project

I also led a team of 13 interns defining the SWAP-C constraints for a 1U server rack. 1U is one rack unit, 1.75 inches tall, so everything has to fit in a very thin box, and every one of the four letters pulls against the others.

<TeamDots count={13} label="13 interns, one lead. I ran the team that worked out the constraints." />

| | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Size** | Fitting into a single 1U slot in a standard rack |
| **Weight** | What the rack and the ship can carry |
| **Power** | What the unit draws and the heat it has to get rid of in a tight space |
| **Cost** | Keeping the whole thing buildable within a budget |
