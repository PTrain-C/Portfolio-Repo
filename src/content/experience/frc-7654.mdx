## Lead Programmer, 2022 to 2023

I wrote the teleoperated and autonomous code for the robot in Java.

## President and captain, 2023 to 2024

I ran the team and kept programming. We finished 25th of 45 teams at the Las Vegas Regional, the highest placement the team had reached.

## Lead Mentor, 2024 to 2025

After high school I came back for a year as a volunteer lead mentor, to help the team move to new leadership. I guided the programming subteam, reviewed power distribution and wire routing layouts, and helped draft competition design concepts.
