QEE is a USC student organization focused on quantum education and the societal side of quantum technology: law, policy, business, and the arts as well as computing, for students without a physics or CS background. It's the only quantum-focused student org on campus. "Programming" in my title means planning events, not writing code. It's an outreach and leadership role.

I plan lab tours, a professor panel series, and Qiskit workshops that lead into our hackathon.

## Q³ Qompute @ USC (2025)

Southern California's first quantum hackathon, run as part of the Q³ Initiative, a joint effort with the University of Maryland supported by the Center for Quantum Networks and the UN International Telecommunication Union (ITU). IBM Quantum worked directly with the team and built custom Qiskit challenges for people without a physics or CS background. There were two keynotes and a closing panel with USC faculty. I helped with logistics and helped secure $15K+ in sponsorships. Strong feedback from participants and sponsors led directly to a second event.

## Qompute 2026

Sunday, October 4, 2026 at USC, with a virtual option. QEE is running this one on its own. It isn't part of the Q³ Initiative and isn't a University of Maryland collaboration, but it is an official IBM Qiskit Fall Fest event (Partner Plus). Teams of up to 4 work on IBM Quantum challenges, with workshops beforehand, keynotes and a panel across physics, engineering, policy, and industry, a company expo, and a closing dinner. I drafted sponsorship outreach.
